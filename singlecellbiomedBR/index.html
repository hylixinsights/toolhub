Brazilian science, read through PubMed

48,676 Brazilian researchers, profiled like single cells

We linked the journal articles listed in the Lattes CVs of Brazilian PhD holders to PubMed, counted the MeSH terms on each researcher's articles, and analysed the result with the toolkit built for single-cell RNA-seq. Each researcher is a cell. Each MeSH term is a gene.

Concept by Helder Nakaya · CSBL

The pipeline
    DataLattes CVs (CNPq) from the BaseD collection, obtained by Prof. Jesus Mena-Chalco's lab. PubMed/MEDLINE records from the U.S. National Library of Medicine.
    ConceptHelder Nakaya conceived the approach of representing researchers as single cells and MeSH terms as genes to map scientific activity.
    Computational Systems Biology Laboratory ↗
    Instagram · @helder_nakaya ↗
    AnalysisJeevan Giddaluru and Helder Nakaya, Computational Systems Biology Laboratory (CSBL).
    SoftwarePython, Scanpy 1.10.3, igraph Leiden, umap-learn 0.5.12. Cluster labels were curated from marker MeSH terms.
